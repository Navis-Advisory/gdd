## Governing thought

Pass at the proposed ~6× ARR: the evidenced levers do not compound to the required 2× ARR by CY2030 — the market lever is refuted (CY2025 in-segment spend below the ~$200M headroom bar in every defensible construction, demand-base growth 1.4–6.0%/yr against the 14.9%/yr required), the share-gain and pricing levers carry no surviving support, and the teaser's own headline numbers cannot jointly hold — so the deal is revisitable only if data-room evidence redefines the in-segment asset and the price is renegotiated to it.

## Key line

Ordered by what the IC will challenge first: the sizing (it contradicts the client's "under-penetrated" prior), then the teaser arithmetic (it contradicts the "#2 player" prior), then displacement, pricing, and the risk backdrop.

1. **The market lever fails.** CY2025 NA pest-control FSM spend sits in a $55–116M working span — below the ~$200M headroom bar in every defensible construction from either independent leg (max defensible high $167M) — and the underlying demand base grows at 1.4–6.0%/yr (US; Canada ~2–3%) against the 14.9%/yr the thesis requires. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F5]
2. **The asset being priced is not yet defined.** Teaser ~$40M ARR (~90% NA ⇒ ~$36M) implies a 31–65% share of the sized market — jointly consistent with a #2 rank and the observed vendor set (PestPac est $25–55M alone) only at simultaneous extremes; at most two of {teaser ARR in-segment, #2 rank, F1 span} hold, and every branch of that trilemma is adverse at ~6× ARR. [F8, F9]
3. **Share gain from generic FSM tools is unsupported on the surviving record.** The vertical's top end is consolidator-owned, and the largest "generic" (ServiceTitan) owns pest-compliance capability first-party — so the displacement contest is partly with consolidator-backed direct verticals, not underfeatured horizontals; the genuine feature gap (no horizontal ships comparable native compliance) survives, but the displacement-pool size (H-comp-1) and switching direction (H-comp-3) carry no un-contested ledger support (see Risks & sensitivities). [F13]
5. **The risk backdrop weakens rather than bounds the thesis.** The federal record-keeping driver is refuted (USDA RUP recordkeeping rescission effective 2025-07-11; the driver survives only at the state level and is no longer trending up); roll-up absorption holds only at the count bar (~1%/yr) with revenue-weighted composition unresolved; the no-foreclosure and no-AI-commoditization checks survive only as present-state findings while the structure for both is fully assembled and incumbent-led. **Answer: No — and not marginally.** Lever by lever, gross of retention (SENSITIVITY ARITHMETIC from ledgered inputs, executed):

Even the tier-6, global, calibration-only software CAGR high of 12.5% gives 1.80× < 2×. At the best evidenced base (6.0%/yr), share × pricing must contribute a further 1.49×.
- **Share gain:** carries no un-contested support (key-line claim 3). Even reviving CONTESTED F7 at its high edge, the entire horizontal pool's annual spend recomputes to ~$38M (7.0k operators × the $5.4k/yr implied spend per digitized operator from triangulation D7-1) — doubling the ~$36M NA teaser ARR from displacement alone would require ~100% capture of the pool's spend.
- **Pricing:** the headroom premise is unsupported and in tension with the scale claim (F13).
- **Joint maximum-favorable construction:** granting every disputed reading in the deal's favor simultaneously — market at the TD leg's max defensible high $167M (above the working span), growth at 6.0%/yr → ~$224M by CY2030 — reaching 2× NA ARR ($72M) still requires ~32% share of the grown market (vs ~22% implied today at that size), i.e. share gain against consolidator-owned incumbents, under a construction in which each input sits at an extreme outside its evidenced central range, all at once. That is the only construction under which the thesis is arithmetically expressible, and it is not evidenced.
- **Net of retention:** unanswerable — retention is untested (KQ3 scoped out by the client). Every figure above is gross; any churn raises the required gross adds further. **The "No" is therefore a ceiling: the levers fail before the untested retention question is even reached.** The memo states this blind spot explicitly (see Risks & sensitivities).
