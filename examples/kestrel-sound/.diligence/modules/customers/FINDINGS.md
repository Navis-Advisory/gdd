review-incentive programs — flagged corpus-wide, retroactive to S38/S39.

multiple independent items, no destination vendor named — product risk,
not competitive departure).

### Tenure and churn-direction tally

**CU2 — tenure/churn tally (→ H-cust-1; promoted F19).** Pest-tagged
subset only (Briostack excluded, tags non-pest — see Open questions).
Tenure-positive n=13 (PestPac/FieldRoutes/GorillaDesk, self-selected +2
vendor-curated case-study items) vs churn/exodus-direction n=11
(self-selected +BBB complainants). Direction only, tier-6-dominant, no
rate. PestPac carries 6 of 11 churn items, **cross-corroborated across
three independent channels** (Capterra, Software Advice, BBB): disputed
contract terms, a documented Oct-2024 price hike (PestPac Lite $250→
$600/mo), post-cancellation billing to collections, "$40k to leave" — a
contract/billing pattern, not confirmed product abandonment; no
comparable cluster at FieldRoutes or GorillaDesk (whose cluster is
product-quality, not contract-driven). Kill-signal search: no further
churn-wave clustering found; no vertical→horizontal departure (corroborates
F12). Reads as "renewal exists, but partly involuntary at the segment's
largest named vertical peer (F8)" — a quality caveat, not a magnitude one.

### KPC table

**CU3 — ranked KPC table (→ H-cust-2; promoted F20).** Multi-platform
(Capterra, Software Advice, BBB, G2-snippet, vendor case studies — no
platform dominates):

| Rank | Criterion | n | Direction | Buyer language (quoted) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Price/cost | ≥8 | both (battleground) | win: "catered more to pest control" (cost-driven); loss (PestPac): "$1,200 to get out of this contract" |
| 2 | Support/service | ≥6 | both (battleground) | win: "superior customer service"; loss: "used to solve issues quickly, but now..." |
| 3 | Ease of use / complexity | ≥5 | both (size-dependent battleground) | win: "chose GorillaDesk for simplicity"; loss (PestPac): "not flexible enough to use for growth" |

Excluded from ranking (category-wide, not differentiating): billing/
contract friction, n≥9, loss-direction, every vendor incl. horizontals.
Thin/no evidence: mobile app (n=1, secondhand); reporting/routing (n=0 as
a stated switching reason). Reverse-flow re-checked independently (3
searches): **zero items**, corroborating but not resolving F12's
CONTESTED status — Reddit and mypmp.net, the likeliest neutral channels,
were environment-blocked this session (see Open questions).

**Axes re-check flag:** C2's positioning axes (compliance depth × operator
size) were analyst-derived before this table existed. Compliance is a
real, uncontested win-driver but not the dominant criterion by volume —
price/support out-frequency it and are both battlegrounds. A revised axis
should weight price/support at least equally with compliance depth.

### Expansion-mechanism scan

**CU4 — pricing units and modules (→ H-cust-3; promoted F21).** Confirmed
on live official pricing pages (direct-fetch, 2026-07-11): GorillaDesk
prices per technician-schedule with an explicit incremental charge
(+$50/schedule) plus SMS/VoIP add-ons; PestPac's tier names encode
branch/location and commercial-size scaling with 5 named add-on modules
(Route Optimization, Termite/WDI-WDO, Advanced Reporting, Marketing
Automation, Lead Management); FieldRoutes bills "based on the number of
active customers" with 2 named add-ons (Marketing Pro, Fleet Pro).
Confirms clause (≥2 vendors) clears on 3. Attach/upgrade testimony
thinner: a GorillaDesk reviewer paying the higher tier for one feature
("I have to pay $99 instead of $50... the only thing I use that upgrade
for is documents"); a FieldRoutes reviewer's real invoice (~$364/mo, <500
customers) consistent with the volume-tier model; a case study describing
technician/vehicle scaling (spend link inferred, not stated). Briostack
has no public pricing/module page found anywhere (gap). Wayback Machine
fetch is environment-blocked this session (BoC/Fed-class block) — live
pages substituted.
