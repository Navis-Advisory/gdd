**M3 — H-comp-2 arithmetic (→ H-comp-2; promoted F8).** Stated with
the labeled assumption ARR ≈ in-segment spend (defined_terms OPEN —
same handling as H-mkt-1):

- Teaser ~$40M ARR × ~90% NA = **~$36M NA in-segment**.
- Against F1 ($55–116M span, L, IRRECONCILED): implied share **31%
  (at span top) to 65% (at span low) of the $55–116M span**.
- For a #2 rank, exactly one player must be larger: only PestPac (est
  $25–55M pest-software revenue, market module E5′ basis) plausibly
  qualifies, and only at the top of its range (>$36M). Top-2 then
  ≥$72M — already >100% of span-low, 85% of span-mid ($85M), 62% of
  span-top.
- Remaining named set at estimate floors (FieldRoutes 18, GorillaDesk
  4, Briostack 3, fringe 2, horizontals-in-segment 6) adds ≥$33M →
  total ≥$105M, ≈ span top with zero fringe room.
- **Conclusion: the joint claim {teaser ARR in-segment, #2 rank, F1
  span} survives only at simultaneous extremes — denominator at the
  irreconciled span's top AND PestPac above $36M AND every other
  vendor at its estimate floor. At most two of the three claims
  plausibly hold**: either the teaser overstates / includes
  out-of-segment revenue (payments, other verticals), or the rank
  claim fails, or F1 materially understates the market (possible —
  F1 is confidence L — but the BU leg would then be wrong in a
  direction its own supplier cross-check contradicts).

### Horizontal feature audit

**M4 — native-core present state (→ H-comp-4; promoted F10).** As of
2026-07:
- **Jobber**: ships and markets "chemical tracking" (S35) — but it is
  a custom-job-forms layer (technician fills a form; history
  exportable). No state report formats, no WDI/WDO templates, no bait
  station tracking, no EPA-registration validation structures (S35,
  S36). Closest any horizontal comes; not functionally comparable.
- **Housecall Pro**: no pest-compliance capability; pest is a minor
  vertical in an HVAC/plumbing/cleaning base (S36).
- **ServiceTitan core**: no native EPA/chemical-usage/state-form
  support; pest supported but not optimized — enterprise pest
  operators reportedly layer FieldRoutes for field compliance with
  ServiceTitan for reporting/finance (S36).
Confirms-clause satisfied: no major horizontal ships native
pest-compliance functionally comparable to GorillaDesk / PestPac /
Fieldwork-class logging (pesticide, EPA reg no., rate, area, target
pest, per-property, audit-exportable).

**M5 — kill-clause contradiction (→ H-comp-4; promoted F11).** The
kill clause ("shipped or formally announced… incl. via first-party
acquisition") is literally satisfied: ServiceTitan acquired ServicePro/
ServSuite (Jan 2021) and FieldRoutes (Jan 2022) (S23, S37) — the
largest horizontal owns leading pest-compliance capability outright.
The brief's own set construction simultaneously classes FieldRoutes as
a direct vertical. Both readings are reported as a contradiction in
the brief's terms rather than resolved: substantively, the horizontal
threat did not "close the gap" in core product — it bought the
vertical — which reframes C2's displacement leg: share gain "from
generic tools" is partly share contest with ServiceTitan-owned
FieldRoutes, a consolidator-backed direct competitor, not an
underfeatured generic.
